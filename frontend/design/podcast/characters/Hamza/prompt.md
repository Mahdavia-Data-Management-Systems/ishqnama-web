# STYLE
Warm, friendly stylised character illustration for a modern Quran reading app, in the manner of contemporary animated-film character design: softly rounded shapes, clean confident linework, gentle cel shading with one soft key light from the upper left and a subtle warm rim light, smooth skin with no texture, large expressive eyes, slightly simplified features. Not photorealistic, not anime, not chibi, not flat corporate vector. Dignified and approachable.

# PALETTE
Accents only in deep teal (#004446), bright teal (#107E8D) and muted gold (#BEAA30); everything else in natural warm skin and fabric tones. Background is a single flat, even colour: pure white (#FBF3D0), no gradient, no vignette, no pattern.

# FRAMING
Head-and-shoulders bust portrait, square 1:1 canvas, face centred, top of head about 10% below the top edge, shoulders cut cleanly at the bottom edge, generous empty margin on both sides so the image survives a circular crop. Front-facing, eyes to camera, as if speaking to the viewer in a video call.

# RULES
No text, no logo, no watermark, no frame, no border, no glow, no halo, no light rays, no sparkles, no props in hand, no religious symbols, no Arabic calligraphy, no background objects. Modest clothing covering arms and chest fully.

# CHARACTER
Hamza, a 24-year-old South Asian man from Hyderabad, India. Medium-brown skin, kind dark eyes, neat short black hair swept slightly to the side, a short, tidy, well-groomed beard. Thin round gold-rimmed glasses. Calm, warm half-smile, the look of someone who enjoys explaining things patiently. Wearing a deep teal (#004446) kurta with a plain round collar, under an open light-grey casual bomber jacket. A thin gold (#BEAA30) line of embroidery along the kurta collar is the only ornament.

# MOOD
Composed, reassuring, gently confident. He is the one who has read the book and is happy to walk you through it.
